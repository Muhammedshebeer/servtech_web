Industrial Instrumentation Services

Instrumentation Solutions for Accurate Process Monitoring and Control

Servtech provides industrial instrumentation support for equipment used in pressure, temperature, flow, level and process control applications.

Our services help plant operators maintain instrument functionality, improve measurement reliability and support process equipment maintenance, installation and commissioning activities.

From field instrument replacement and troubleshooting to calibration and control valve accessories, Servtech supports instrumentation work across power generation, water treatment, desalination and industrial utility systems.

Scope of services

  • Field instrument installation and replacement.
  • Instrument inspection and troubleshooting.
  • Pressure, temperature, level and flow instrument servicing.
  • Instrument calibration and functional verification.
  • Pressure and differential pressure transmitter maintenance.
  • Temperature sensor and transmitter servicing.
  • Level transmitter and level switch maintenance.
  • Control valve positioner inspection and calibration.
  • Instrument air tubing and connection works.
  • Instrument manifold and impulse-line maintenance.
  • Instrument signal testing and loop checks.
  • 4–20 mA signal verification.
  • HART instrument configuration support.
  • Instrument cable termination and testing.
  • Instrument commissioning and start-up support.
  • Preventive and corrective instrumentation maintenance.

Equipment & systems supported

Pressure transmitters, differential pressure transmitters, temperature transmitters, RTDs, thermocouples, level instruments, process switches, instrument manifolds, control valve positioners and associated field devices.

Supply & products

Pressure, temperature, level and flow transmitters, gauges, switches, level gauges, RTDs, thermocouples, control valve positioners, instrument manifolds, fittings, tubing, signal converters and field instrumentation accessories.
