Technical products for maintenance, projects and plant upgrades.

In addition to our specialist maintenance and engineering services, Servtech supports industrial procurement requirements through the supply and sourcing of valves, instrumentation, mechanical components, industrial piping materials and associated spare parts.

Our supply solutions are intended to support new installations, preventive maintenance, equipment refurbishment, plant upgrades, and shutdown requirements.

By combining engineering service experience with technical product selection, we help customers identify suitable components based on their equipment specifications, materials, pressure ratings, temperature requirements and intended applications.
