Mechanical Maintenance and Valve Solutions for Marine Applications

Servtech supports marine and offshore-related mechanical maintenance requirements, with a particular focus on valves, pressure systems, boilers, piping, instrumentation and equipment refurbishment.

Our service offering is relevant to ship repair companies, shipyards, marine engineering contractors, port facilities, offshore support operators, and related industrial service providers.

Through workshop-based servicing and project-specific field maintenance support, Servtech can assist with the repair and restoration of equipment used in marine utility, steam, cooling, compressed air and fluid handling systems.

Key systems supported

Marine boilers, steam piping, seawater cooling circuits, ballast and bilge piping, compressed air systems, pressure safety valves, pumps and associated mechanical equipment.

Relevant Servtech services

  • Valve refurbishment
  • PSV servicing and testing
  • Marine boiler mechanical maintenance
  • Piping fabrication
  • Precision machining
  • Pump maintenance
  • Instrument calibration
  • Technical manpower support

Products & spares

Marine application valves, safety relief valves, pressure gauges, pipe fittings, boiler accessories, mechanical seals, gaskets, instrumentation, and fabricated mechanical components.

Marine qualification note

Any work requiring classification society approvals, shipboard statutory certification, or specific marine authorizations must be described according to Servtech's actual approval status and the requirements of the vessel or operator.
