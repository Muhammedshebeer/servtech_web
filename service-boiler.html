HRSG & Auxiliary Boiler Maintenance

Comprehensive Boiler Maintenance for Reliable Steam Generation

Servtech provides mechanical maintenance, inspection, repair and overhaul services for Heat Recovery Steam Generators (HRSGs), auxiliary boilers and industrial steam-generation systems.

Supporting combined-cycle power plants, cogeneration facilities, desalination plants and industrial utility operations, our services are structured to maintain boiler reliability, improve operational performance and support safe return to service following planned or corrective maintenance.

From routine inspection and pressure-part repairs to major boiler outages, Servtech provides skilled mechanical teams and supporting technical resources for critical boiler maintenance activities.

Scope of services

  • HRSG and auxiliary boiler inspection and maintenance.
  • Planned boiler shutdown and annual overhaul support.
  • Boiler pressure-part inspection and repair.
  • Boiler tube inspection, repair and replacement.
  • Economizer and superheater maintenance.
  • Steam drum and header maintenance.
  • Boiler internal inspection and cleaning.
  • Burner and combustion-system mechanical maintenance.
  • Fuel gas piping and related valve maintenance.
  • Boiler ducting and expansion-joint maintenance.
  • Boiler casing, insulation and refractory maintenance.
  • Feedwater, steam and condensate system maintenance.
  • Boiler safety valve removal, servicing and testing.
  • Boiler piping repair and replacement.
  • Welding, fabrication and pressure testing.
  • Mechanical reinstatement and recommissioning assistance.

Equipment & systems supported

HRSGs, auxiliary boilers, steam drums, economizers, superheaters, reheaters, headers, boiler feedwater systems, blowdown systems, combustion equipment, steam piping and associated mechanical auxiliaries.

Supply & spare parts

Boiler tubes and bends, economizer and superheater components, boiler valves, steam accessories, expansion joints, bellows, gaskets, insulation, refractory materials, piping fittings, fasteners and burner-related mechanical components.
