Safety Valve Bench & Online Testing

Accurate Safety Valve Testing for Safe and Reliable Plant Operation

Servtech provides both workshop-based bench testing and specialized online safety valve testing for industrial pressure protection systems.

Our testing services support scheduled maintenance, statutory inspection programs, boiler outages, pressure system verification and plant reliability requirements.

Through bench testing and suitable online test methods, we help plant operators evaluate the operating condition of safety valves and identify valves that require adjustment, servicing or replacement.

Bench testing services

Bench testing is performed with the safety valve removed from its installation and mounted on an appropriate test arrangement.

Bench testing scope

  • As-received set-pressure testing.
  • Set-pressure adjustment and verification.
  • Seat leakage testing.
  • Pop and reseating observations, where applicable.
  • Air, gas or liquid testing as permitted by valve design and approved procedure.
  • Post-overhaul testing and documentation.
  • Identification and reporting of failed safety valves.

Online safety valve testing

Online testing uses an appropriate lifting-assist or force-measurement method to assess the valve's opening characteristics while installed on an operating pressure system.

Online testing scope

  • Online testing of suitable spring-operated safety valves.
  • Applied-force measurement and set-pressure evaluation.
  • Testing during normal plant operating conditions, where permitted.
  • Valve performance evaluation.
  • Recording and analysis of test results.
  • Identification of valves requiring corrective maintenance.
  • Preparation of test records and technical recommendations.

Technical distinction

Online testing is not universally suitable for every valve and does not necessarily replace a full bench-based functional or capacity test. The testing method must be approved for the valve design, operating conditions and applicable inspection requirements.
