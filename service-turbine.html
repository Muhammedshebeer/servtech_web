Servtech services

Turbine & Rotating Equipment Maintenance

Precision Mechanical Maintenance for Critical Rotating Machinery

Servtech supports planned and corrective maintenance activities for turbines and associated rotating equipment used in power generation and industrial utility applications.

Our mechanical maintenance services focus on equipment condition assessment, dismantling, component inspection, mechanical restoration, alignment, assembly and recommissioning assistance.

We support plant maintenance teams during routine servicing, equipment overhauls and scheduled outages, with work packages defined around equipment condition, technical specifications and specialist support requirements.

Scope of services

  • Steam and gas turbine mechanical maintenance support.
  • Turbine dismantling and reassembly assistance.
  • Rotor and mechanical component inspection.
  • Bearing and seal maintenance.
  • Mechanical cleaning and component assessment.
  • Shaft alignment and coupling inspection.
  • Pump overhaul and refurbishment.
  • Compressor mechanical maintenance support.
  • Fan and blower maintenance.
  • Gearbox inspection and maintenance.
  • Lubrication system mechanical maintenance.
  • Mechanical seal and bearing replacement.
  • Vibration investigation and balancing support.
  • Equipment installation and replacement.
  • Pre-commissioning and start-up assistance.

Equipment supported

Steam turbines, gas turbine mechanical auxiliaries, centrifugal pumps, industrial compressors, blowers, fans, gearboxes, bearings, seals, couplings and associated rotating machinery.

Supply & spare parts

Bearings, mechanical seals, couplings, flexible elements, shafts, sleeves, gaskets, fasteners, pump and compressor service parts and mechanical components manufactured against approved drawings or samples.
