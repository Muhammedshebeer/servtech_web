Servtech services

Shutdown, Turnaround & Outage Support

Coordinated Maintenance Support for Planned and Critical Plant Outages

Servtech provides mechanical maintenance and multidisciplinary technical support for planned shutdowns, industrial turnarounds, power plant outages and major equipment overhaul programs.

Our services are designed to support plant operators in completing maintenance activities within defined shutdown windows while maintaining a strong focus on safety, work quality, technical coordination and equipment readiness.

Through deployment of specialist maintenance teams, supervision, tools and supporting resources, Servtech assists customers throughout shutdown preparation, equipment servicing, testing, mechanical reinstatement and start-up activities.

Scope of services

  • Shutdown work scope assessment and planning support.
  • Site surveys and equipment condition assessment.
  • Preparation of work packs and execution sequences.
  • Technical manpower and supervision deployment.
  • Industrial valve dismantling, refurbishment and testing.
  • Pressure safety valve servicing and testing.
  • HRSG and auxiliary boiler overhaul support.
  • Turbine and rotating equipment maintenance.
  • Industrial piping modification and replacement.
  • Fabrication and mechanical erection.
  • Instrument calibration and functional checks.
  • Electrical maintenance support.
  • Pressure testing and mechanical completion support.
  • Punch-list closure and equipment reinstatement.
  • Maintenance documentation and final handover reports.

Shutdown services for different facilities

  • Power generation plants — HRSG, boiler, steam valve, turbine auxiliary and safety valve maintenance.
  • Desalination plants — High-pressure piping, valve overhauls, pumps and instrumentation.
  • Heavy industrial plants — Steam utilities, process piping, mechanical equipment and valves.
  • Marine and shipyard facilities — Marine valves, pressure systems, boiler auxiliaries and piping.

Support arrangements

Project-based specialist teams, defined maintenance work packages, shutdown manpower deployment, workshop refurbishment and on-site maintenance services.

Key industries
