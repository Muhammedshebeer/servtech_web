Specialized Maintenance Solutions for Power Generation Facilities

Servtech supports power generation facilities with mechanical maintenance, valve refurbishment, boiler servicing, safety valve testing, equipment repairs, instrumentation and outage maintenance services.

Our industry experience is particularly relevant to combined-cycle power plants, conventional steam power stations, cogeneration facilities and industrial captive power plants.

We understand the importance of plant availability, steam system integrity, pressure protection and coordinated maintenance within scheduled outage periods.

Key systems supported

HRSGs, auxiliary boilers, steam turbines, gas turbine auxiliaries, main steam systems, feedwater systems, condensate systems, fuel gas piping, cooling water systems, pressure relief systems, pumps and mechanical auxiliaries.

Relevant Servtech services

  • Valve refurbishment and testing
  • PSV bench and online testing
  • Boiler maintenance
  • Turbine and rotating equipment support
  • Machining
  • Piping fabrication
  • Instrumentation
  • Calibration
  • Electrical maintenance
  • Shutdown support

Products & spares

Steam valves, pressure safety valves, valve internals, boiler tubes, expansion joints, steam system accessories, pressure and temperature instruments and rotating equipment spares.
