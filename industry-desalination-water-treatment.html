Mechanical, Valve and Instrumentation Services for Water Infrastructure

Servtech supports desalination plants, water treatment facilities, pumping stations and industrial water networks with maintenance services covering valves, process piping, pumps, instrumentation, and flow measurement equipment.

Our solutions address the maintenance requirements of Reverse Osmosis (RO), Multi-Stage Flash (MSF), Multi-Effect Distillation (MED), and associated water transfer and utility systems.

We focus on maintaining the performance of equipment exposed to demanding operating conditions, including high pressures, saline water, corrosion, and continuous plant operation.

Key systems supported

Seawater intake and discharge systems, RO high-pressure systems, water treatment lines, pumping stations, industrial valves, cooling water networks, chemical dosing systems and water distribution infrastructure.

Relevant Servtech services

  • Industrial valve refurbishment
  • Valve testing
  • Fabrication and piping
  • Pump maintenance
  • Calibration
  • Instrumentation
  • Flow measurement
  • Electrical support
  • Mechanical maintenance and shutdown works

Products & spares

Duplex and super duplex valves, butterfly valves, plug valves, check valves, high-pressure isolation valves, control valves, flowmeters, pressure instruments, expansion joints and suitable corrosion-resistant piping components.
