Flow Measurement & Metering Services

Dependable Flow Measurement Solutions for Water, Steam and Industrial Processes

Servtech provides flow measurement and metering support for industrial applications where accurate measurement is essential for process monitoring, water distribution, operational control and system performance.

Our proposed services encompass flowmeter inspection, installation, replacement, configuration, verification, troubleshooting and integration with field instrumentation systems.

We support the selection and maintenance of flow measurement instruments based on process fluid, pipe size, operating pressure, temperature, conductivity, measurement range and application-specific accuracy requirements.

Scope of services

  • Flowmeter installation and replacement.
  • Flowmeter condition assessment and troubleshooting.
  • Flow measurement instrument verification.
  • Flow transmitter configuration and functional testing.
  • Electromagnetic flowmeter maintenance support.
  • Ultrasonic flowmeter installation and servicing.
  • Clamp-on ultrasonic flow measurement.
  • Differential pressure flow measurement inspection.
  • Flowmeter signal verification and loop checking.
  • Flowmeter removal, reinstallation and recommissioning.
  • Metering instrument maintenance programs.
  • Flow measurement system documentation.
  • Flowmeter calibration coordination, where required.

Flowmeter technologies

  • Electromagnetic flowmeters.
  • Ultrasonic flowmeters.
  • Clamp-on ultrasonic flowmeters.
  • Vortex flowmeters.
  • Turbine flowmeters.
  • Differential pressure flow measurement devices.
  • Other application-specific industrial metering instruments.

Applications

Raw water, treated water, desalinated water, cooling water, chilled water, condensate, industrial utilities and suitable steam or process-fluid measurement applications.

Supply & products

Electromagnetic, ultrasonic and clamp-on flowmeters, flow transmitters, indicators, sensors, transducers, installation accessories, differential pressure flow elements and metering-system components.
