Safety Valve Repair, Overhaul Setting & Certification

Protecting Critical Pressure Systems Through Reliable Safety Valve Maintenance

Servtech delivers inspection, servicing, repair, overhaul and set-pressure adjustment for pressure safety valves and pressure relief valves installed across industrial plants and utility systems.

Safety valves are critical protective devices designed to safeguard personnel, equipment and pressure systems against excessive operating pressure. Effective maintenance is essential to preserve their intended protective function and support safe plant operation.

Our safety valve maintenance services focus on restoring mechanical condition, verifying valve performance and confirming operational readiness in accordance with approved procedures and applicable requirements.

Scope of services

  • Initial visual inspection and condition assessment.
  • As-received testing, where technically feasible.
  • Dismantling and complete internal inspection.
  • Spring, spindle, nozzle, disc and guide inspection.
  • Cleaning, polishing and seat lapping.
  • Replacement of defective components and soft parts.
  • Spring condition verification and adjustment.
  • Set-pressure adjustment and testing.
  • Seat tightness and leakage testing.
  • Reassembly and functional verification.
  • Safety valve tagging, sealing and reporting.
  • Workshop and on-site overhaul support.
  • Periodic servicing under preventive maintenance programs.

Types of safety valves

Conventional spring-loaded safety valves, balanced-bellows safety relief valves and pilot-operated pressure relief valves, subject to model-specific service capability.

Typical applications

HRSG and auxiliary boiler steam systems, superheaters, economizers, pressure vessels, compressed air receivers, fuel gas systems, industrial steam networks and marine boilers.

Supply & spare parts

Pressure safety and relief valves, replacement springs, nozzles, discs, seats, spindle assemblies, bellows assemblies, seals, gaskets, repair kits and safety valve accessories.
