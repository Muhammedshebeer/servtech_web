Servtech services

Precision Machining & Reverse Engineering

Restoring Critical Components Through Precision Engineering

Servtech provides machining, component refurbishment and reverse engineering services to support industrial maintenance, equipment restoration and replacement of worn or obsolete mechanical parts.

Our capabilities support the repair and manufacture of selected mechanical components for valves, pumps, turbines, boilers, piping assemblies and other industrial equipment.

By combining dimensional inspection, engineering assessment and precision machining, we help customers identify practical repair or replacement solutions, particularly where original components are damaged, obsolete or subject to extended procurement lead times.

Scope of services

  • Conventional and CNC machining, subject to component requirements.
  • Turning, milling, drilling, boring and grinding.
  • Valve seat and sealing-surface machining.
  • Valve stem and spindle refurbishment.
  • Shaft and sleeve manufacturing and repair.
  • Flange-facing and sealing-surface restoration.
  • Reverse engineering of mechanical components.
  • Dimensional inspection and measurement.
  • Preparation of manufacturing drawings and 3D models.
  • Manufacture of components from approved samples and drawings.
  • Mechanical component modification and restoration.
  • Inspection and dimensional verification of completed parts.

Components supported

Valve stems, discs, seats, shafts, sleeves, bushes, spacers, flanges, mechanical adapters, equipment supports and application-specific mechanical parts.

Supply & manufactured products

Custom-machined components, replacement valve internals, shafts, sleeves, bushings, adapters, special fasteners, mechanical fittings and maintenance spares manufactured to approved specifications.
