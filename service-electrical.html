Electrical Maintenance & Technical Services

Electrical Maintenance Solutions for Industrial Reliability and Safety

Servtech offers electrical maintenance and technical support solutions for industrial equipment, auxiliary systems and plant electrical installations.

The proposed electrical service portfolio covers inspection, troubleshooting, maintenance, installation and testing of electrical systems associated with mechanical equipment, process instrumentation, pumps, valves and industrial utilities.

Services are planned around plant operating requirements, electrical safety procedures, equipment specifications and applicable project standards.

Scope of services

  • Industrial electrical installation and maintenance.
  • Electrical equipment inspection and troubleshooting.
  • Electric motor inspection and maintenance.
  • Motor Control Centre (MCC) maintenance support.
  • Electrical control panel inspection and servicing.
  • Cable installation, termination and testing.
  • Electrical continuity and insulation resistance testing.
  • Motor starter and associated control circuit inspection.
  • Electrical actuator inspection and maintenance.
  • Electrical isolation and reinstatement support.
  • Lighting and industrial auxiliary electrical works.
  • Electrical fault identification and corrective maintenance.
  • Electrical support during shutdowns and equipment replacement.
  • Commissioning and functional testing assistance.

Equipment & systems supported

Industrial motors, MCC panels, local control panels, electrical actuators, distribution auxiliaries, cabling systems, motor starters and field electrical equipment.

Supply & products

Electric motors and accessories, electrical control components, switches, relays, motor starters, contactors, cabling, cable accessories, electrical actuators, panel components and industrial sensors.
