Industrial Fabrication, Piping & Erection

Engineered Fabrication and Installation Solutions for Industrial Facilities

Servtech provides industrial metal fabrication, piping works, structural modification, equipment installation and mechanical erection services for new and existing industrial facilities.

Our fabrication and installation support covers maintenance-related modifications, equipment replacement, piping extensions, plant upgrades and shutdown activities.

We undertake works based on approved technical drawings, material specifications, inspection requirements and site safety procedures.

Scope of services

  • Carbon steel and stainless steel piping fabrication.
  • Industrial pipe spool fabrication and installation.
  • Utility and process piping modification.
  • Steam, condensate, water and compressed air piping.
  • Pipeline component replacement and modification.
  • Structural steel fabrication and erection.
  • Industrial platforms, walkways, supports and access structures.
  • Pipe racks and pipe support fabrication.
  • Equipment skids, base frames and mechanical supports.
  • Pumps, valves and associated equipment installation.
  • Mechanical dismantling and reinstallation.
  • Welding, fit-up, alignment and assembly.
  • Flange joint preparation and reinstatement.
  • Pressure testing and inspection support.
  • Installation documentation and as-built updates.

Materials

Carbon steel, stainless steel and other project-approved alloys in accordance with applicable design, material and welding specifications.

Supply & materials

Pipes, fittings, flanges, reducers, elbows, structural steel, fabricated assemblies, pipe supports, clamps, fasteners, gaskets, skids, expansion joints, flexible connectors, valves and piping accessories.
