About Servtech

Experience that keeps industry running.

Our company

A UAE mechanical services partner with a practical mindset.

Servtech Technical Services LLC provides specialized mechanical, electrical, instrumentation, calibration and industrial maintenance solutions for critical industrial facilities. With particular expertise in power generation, desalination, heavy industries and marine applications, Servtech supports plant operators, utility companies, EPC contractors and industrial asset owners throughout equipment maintenance, refurbishment, testing, installation, commissioning and planned shutdowns. Our services encompass industrial valve refurbishment and testing, pressure safety valve servicing, HRSG and auxiliary boiler maintenance, turbine and rotating equipment maintenance, fabrication and piping, precision machining, instrumentation, electrical works, flow measurement, calibration, and technical manpower support. Combining workshop capabilities, experienced field-service personnel, and practical engineering solutions, Servtech helps customers improve equipment reliability, maintain operational safety, minimize unplanned downtime, and extend the service life of critical assets. From individual equipment servicing to major plant outages and long-term maintenance contracts, we deliver coordinated technical support tailored to each facility's operational requirements.
