Industrial Valve Refurbishment, Repair & Testing

Reliable Valve Performance Through Specialist Maintenance and Testing

Servtech provides comprehensive refurbishment, repair, inspection, servicing and pressure testing of industrial valves used in critical process and utility systems.

Our services support steam, water, condensate, fuel gas, compressed air, cooling water and industrial process applications. We handle valve maintenance requirements from routine workshop servicing to planned shutdown activities and emergency corrective maintenance.

Our refurbishment process begins with valve condition assessment and fault identification, followed by dismantling, component inspection, cleaning, repair, machining and replacement of damaged parts, reassembly and final performance testing.

Services can be carried out at our workshop or at customer facilities, depending on the valve type, operating conditions and site requirements.

Scope of services

  • Complete valve inspection, dismantling, overhaul and refurbishment.
  • Valve seat and disc lapping, grinding and restoration.
  • Stem, spindle, bonnet, gland and packing refurbishment.
  • Replacement of gaskets, seals, packing, fasteners and internal components.
  • Valve body and trim condition assessment.
  • Hydrostatic shell and seat leakage testing.
  • Pneumatic pressure and functional testing, where applicable.
  • Valve actuator and gearbox maintenance.
  • Manual, pneumatic and electrically actuated valve support.
  • Valve installation, removal, replacement and recommissioning.
  • Workshop and on-site valve maintenance.
  • Inspection reports, test documentation and service recommendations.

Types of valves

Gate valves, globe valves, ball valves, butterfly valves, check valves, plug valves, non-return valves, control valves, pressure-seal valves and other industrial isolation or regulating valves.

Supply & spare parts

Valve assemblies, stems, discs, seats, gaskets, seals, gland packing, bolting, gear operators, pneumatic and electric actuators, replacement valve trim and manufactured components.
