Industrial Calibration & Certification Services

Reliable Measurement Through Accurate Calibration and Traceable Results

Servtech provides calibration, testing and verification services for industrial measuring instruments used in operation, maintenance, inspection, quality control and process monitoring.

Our calibration services help customers evaluate measurement accuracy, identify deviations, support planned calibration intervals and maintain reliable records for their instrument assets.

Services are structured around instrument type, measurement range, required accuracy, operating environment and applicable calibration procedures.

We support both workshop-based calibration and on-site calibration activities where the equipment and test methods are suitable.

Scope of services

  • Pressure gauge and pressure transmitter calibration.
  • Differential pressure instrument calibration.
  • Vacuum gauge calibration.
  • Temperature gauge and transmitter calibration.
  • Thermocouple and RTD verification.
  • Temperature switch testing.
  • Pressure switch testing.
  • Digital and analog instrument calibration.
  • Electrical measuring instrument calibration.
  • Torque wrench calibration.
  • Gas detection instrument calibration.
  • Instrument inspection and functional checks.
  • As-found and as-left calibration reporting.
  • Instrument identification, tagging and record management.
  • Periodic calibration programs and annual maintenance contracts.

Instruments supported

Pressure gauges, digital pressure indicators, pressure transmitters, temperature gauges, RTDs, thermocouples, pressure switches, temperature switches, digital multimeters, clamp meters, torque wrenches and selected industrial measuring devices.

Supply & products

Pressure gauges and transmitters, temperature gauges and sensors, RTDs, thermocouples, pressure and temperature switches, digital indicators, portable measurement instruments, instrument accessories and replacement parts.

Important

Calibration certificates should only be described as traceable or accredited where the relevant measurement scope, laboratory accreditation and certificate conditions support that claim.
